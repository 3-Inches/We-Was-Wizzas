## A. The app and the book disagree

### A1. Warping from studying vis does not go through `gainWarping`, so Twilight is never owed — confirmed
- A double botch studying raw vis "may enter Wizard's Twilight" (Definitive Edition, line 16215). The toolkit writes the botch's Warping Points onto the season entry, and `applySeason` adds them to the sheet (`src/engine/longterm.ts:237`). `gainWarping` is never called, so nothing waits in Resolve.
- Two places do this: a planned season at its end (`src/engine/time.ts:346`), and a season logged by hand on the sheet (`src/ui/character/sheet/AdvancementTab.tsx:259`).
- The result line even says "A double botch: roll for Wizard's Twilight." Roadmap 1.4b's aim was that nobody has to remember that.
- The guard in `src/quality.test.ts:132` looks only for the text `warpingPoints +=`. Writing the points another way passes it.
- Fix (*Mechanical*): give `gainWarping` an option for points already added (say `counted: true`), which owes the reaction without adding the points again. Call it after `applySeason` in both places, so Unlog still takes the points off through the entry. Widen the guard to any change to `warpingPoints` or `entry.warpingPoints` outside `RAW_WARPING`. Add a test: a double botch studying vis owes Twilight.

### A2. The Hermetic Arts and study from vis are offered to any Gifted character — confirmed
- `AdvancementTab.tsx:103`: `canStudyArts = d.isMagus || d.hasGift`.
- *Hedge Magic* line 433: a hedge wizard "is not able to learn any of the Hermetic Arts unless he has his Gift Opened for the Hermetic Arts". Line 846 says the same.
- So a folk witch's season in full opens on "Read a summa · Creo (0)", and **Log season & add 10 xp** would give the witch 10 experience in Creo. "Study raw vis" is offered too.
- Fix (*Mechanical*): `canStudyArts = d.isMagus`, unless the toolkit records a Gift opened to the Arts some other way. The default subject is then the character's own best Ability. A test: a Gifted folk witch is offered no Art.

## B. Systems that do not work together

### B1. Unlog leaves the Twilight a season caused waiting in Resolve — confirmed
- `unlogSeason` takes the season's Warping Points off (`src/engine/unlog.ts:134`). But `gainWarping` pushes the owed reaction with no link to the season (`src/engine/warping.ts:90`), so it stays.
- Rolls a season's work leaves (a woven curse, a ring's first recitation) are dropped on Unlog. The Warping reactions are not.
- Fix (*Mechanical*): record the owed reaction's uid on the season's `undo` when the season's work raises one, and remove it on Unlog.

### B2. The Chronicle tells every new kind of season as ordinary work — confirmed in the browser
- Every season worked through `useSeasonWork` is logged as `'other'` (`src/ui/seasonWork.tsx:49`): the hedge traditions' six, devotion and the mason's. So is holy work (`src/ui/pages/HolyPart.tsx:1443`).
- The Chronicle's phrases (`src/engine/chronicle.ts:99`) have none for `'other'`, and fall back to exposure (`:181`).
- Played through: the folk witch brewed healing potions with 7 pawns of Corpus. The Chronicle said the witch "saw to the daily round of duties".
- The owner asked for exactly this to be flavorful (`../owner.md`, point 9).
- Fix (*Mechanical*): `useSeasonWork` takes a `told` phrase ("brewed potions in the kitchen", "raised the tower another twenty feet", "kept vigil at a saint's shrine"), stored on the entry. The Chronicle prefers it to the activity's stock phrases. A test per card that the phrase is given.

### B3. A character's season in full puts their own work last — confirmed in the browser
- `SeasonFor` (`src/ui/pages/SeasonsPage.tsx:316`) draws the same cards in the same order for everyone: the study planner, the standing activity, priorities, the log, aging, Mysteries, writing, then the character's own season work.
- For the folk witch at 390, the kitchen is about 80% of the way down. Above it: a plan for a Hermetic Art the witch cannot learn (A2), and Initiation, though the witch belongs to no cult.
- Each pull request that moved a card to Seasons (1.3, 1.4, 1.4c) checked its own card. None looked at the page as one character.
- Fix (*Judgment*): order the cards by the character. Their tradition's season work and Lab projects come first. Then the planner and what applies to them. Then the log and aging. A card that cannot apply is hidden, with one line saying why, as the Faith page does.

### B4. Season cards open on a disabled button — confirmed in the browser
- **A season in the kitchen** opens on the folk witch's first power in the list, Animal Ken 0. So the Brew button is disabled with "No Animal vis in the purse" (`src/ui/pages/FolkWitchPart.tsx:161`). The power card at `:66` does the same.
- **A gruagach's season** opens on a tattoo (`src/ui/pages/GruagachPart.tsx:368`) whose default design is beyond the character: "The tattoo cannot be made as designed (above)".
- The last audit's C5 fixed this on the faerie cards. It is the same fault on other cards.
- Fix (*Mechanical*): default to the power with the highest score, and to the first work the character can do. The card checklist now says so.

### B5. The Sympathy Traits card is editable in two places — confirmed
- `SympathyCard` is drawn whole on the sheet (`src/ui/character/sheet/OverviewTab.tsx:232`) and on Dice › Faerie (`src/ui/pages/FaeriePart.tsx:81`). Both can add, remove and set traits.
- This came before stage 1, from *Realms of Power: Faerie*; the last audit missed it.
- Fix (*Mechanical*): keep the card on the sheet. On Dice › Faerie, show the traits, the Faerie Rank and the botch dice in one line, with a link to the sheet. Experience from play still changes them through the rolls.

### B6. A character sent back from a player reports hedge, faerie and holy changes as "nothing the rules count" — confirmed
- `src/engine/troupe.ts:188` lists what changed between the storyguide's copy and the player's. It names Characteristics, Abilities, Arts, Virtues, Warping and so on. Anything else gives one line: "Other details of the sheet differ (nothing the rules count)".
- Sympathy Traits, a hedge tradition's Arts, runes, charms, holy powers and Devotion Points all count, and fall into that line.
- Fix (*Mechanical*): a line for each: "Sympathy Traits", "Learned Arts", "Runes and scripts", "Holy powers", "Devotion Points". Say "other details" without the claim about the rules.

### B7. Quick build cannot make a hedge wizard, a faerie wizard or a magus of the Mysteries — confirmed
- Pull request #65 found it: guided-build answers for Folk Witch, Gruagach, Vitki, Mathematicus and faerie wizardry rarely produced one. #66 worked round it for the tutorials, by giving the practice characters their Virtues free.
- It was never written into the roadmap, so it would have been lost.
- A player who wants such a character, or the owner building one for them, gets no help from Quick build (`../owner.md`, points 11 and 13).
- Fix (*Judgment*): a guided-build answer for each tradition that takes its Social Status and Virtues, and fills its Abilities as the tradition's chapter says. A test per tradition that the character passes the rules check.

### B8. Great buildings and the menagerie roll on the covenant's page — for the owner
- Roadmap 1.4c put them on the covenant's Overview, as the owner decided. Their stress check and breeding rolls are made in play, and `CLAUDE.md` says play happens at the Game Table.
- Not a fault: the owner chose it. Raised so that the choice is made knowing the rule.
- **Decided by the owner (18 November 2026):** rolls happen at the Game Table. The rolls move to Dice; the record stays on the covenant. *Mechanical*, roadmap 5.8.

## W. How the work was done

Stage 1 was run as the roadmap planned: a fresh session for each item, working from `CLAUDE.md`, the design principles, the roadmap and the audit. The owner asked whether the long single session worked better. This section answers from the record.

### What went well
- **Each item did what it said.** All of the last audit's findings are fixed in the code, not only in the text.
- **Rules were checked against the books.** Every rules fix has a test on the book's own example. 2.1 checked 736 of *Hermetic Projects*' "Required Lab Total" lines against the engine.
- **The owner was asked where the choice was theirs** (1.3, 1.4, 1.4c, 1.7b), and the answers were written into the roadmap.
- **The full checks ran:** tutorials and sweeps at both widths, for every pull request that changed the app (#61's after it had merged).
- **Old faults were found and fixed on the way:** the reader and file names with a comma, two devices sharing an id, a lone gruagach Form that broke the page.
- **The docs learned what the sessions learned.** `CLAUDE.md` and the design principles were updated in the same pull requests.

### What slipped, and why

**W1. Nobody looked between systems.** A1, B1, B2 and B3 are each at a join between the item's own card and a system it did not name. A session working one roadmap item reads that item's files. It had no list of the systems that read the same records. The long session knew them from having built them. *Done here:* an integration checklist in the design principles, which the system checklist now requires.

**W2. The owner's expectations were never written down.** The owner spent a month saying how they play and what they want: everything in one place, prompts not memory, a story in the Chronicle, nothing offered that cannot apply. The long session held this in its conversation, and lost some of it each time the conversation was summarized. The fresh sessions had none of it. *Done here:* `../owner.md`, from the owner's own messages, is now first on `CLAUDE.md`'s reading list.

**W3. The sweep did not test the owner's game.** `CLAUDE.md` names no saga for the sweep, so each session swept the default test saga. That saga has 24 characters, 5 of the 22 optional rules and no hedge or faerie characters: 357 pages. The owner plays with every optional rule but one and the whole Tribunal living, a 111-character saga. The new hedge, Faerie and Mysteries pages were seen only through `shots.cjs`. *Done here:* `QA_LIVING=all QA_OPTIONAL=all npm run qa:saga` makes the owner's saga, and `CLAUDE.md` says to sweep that.

**W4. A fault was called intermittent in eight pull requests.** From #54 to #63, a City & Guild tutorial step that found nothing was put down as intermittent and "not this pull request's". #65 traced it: the test script looked for the card before it made the practice craftsman. Each session saw it fresh and had no reason to dig. *Done here:* `CLAUDE.md` says that "intermittent" is not a cause, and that a fault seen twice is traced or written into the roadmap.

**W5. A finding left in a pull request's text is lost.** B7 was found in #65 and written only there. *Done here:* `CLAUDE.md` says that a finding the item does not fix goes into the roadmap, in the same pull request.

**W6. Merging outran the checks.** #61 was merged three minutes after it opened, with "results to follow". #63 brought them. Several others were merged within ten minutes. *Done here:* `CLAUDE.md` says to open the pull request only when every check has finished. For the owner: a pull request is ready to merge when its Checks section is complete.

**W7. The roadmap's own rules made busywork.** "Tick an item when its pull request merges" meant each session ticked the item before it, in its own pull request: #55, #56, #59, #60, #61, #62 and #63 all did, and #63 did little else. "Delete the audit file" would have removed the model that stage 4 says to copy (the owner caught this). *Done here:* an item is ticked in its own pull request, and audits are kept in `app/docs/audits/`.

### Single session, or fresh sessions?

Each has a real strength.

- **A fresh session per item** is cheap and sharp. It reads only what the item needs, and its context is not crowded by a month of history. This audit found no item done carelessly.
- **The long session** carries what was never written: why things are where they are, how systems feed each other, and what the owner meant. It is expensive, and its memory is a summary of a summary, which also loses things. The last audit missed B5 in this very session.

The faults above are not from fresh sessions as such. They are from knowledge that lived only in the conversation. Most of it is now in `../owner.md`, the integration checklist and `CLAUDE.md`.

What remains is review: a builder session checks its own work, and nobody checks the joins before the merge. Recommended:

1. **Builders:** keep a fresh session for each roadmap item.
2. **A lead session, kept long:** the owner's conversation for direction, plans and audits. Before merging a pull request, ask it to review the diff against `../owner.md` and the integration checklist. That is a short read, not a rebuild. What the owner tells it goes into `../owner.md`, so the builders get it too.
3. **An audit after each stage,** as this one, before the next stage starts.
